Tiltwake Support

Questions, bugs or an alarm that didn't ring? Email hello@brilliantdisruptions.com and we'll get back to you.

My alarm didn't ring

Open Tiltwake and look at the Alarm Health card on the Alarms tab. If it's amber, tap it to fix what it shows. The most common causes are alarm permission being turned off (in the Settings app, under Apps → Tiltwake) or the phone being switched off. If the card is green and an alarm still didn't ring, please email us with your iPhone model and iOS version.

I can't solve the maze

A path hint appears after 90 seconds, and an emergency escape unlocks after 3 minutes on every plan. If you can't tilt your phone, turn on the accessible dismiss option in Settings.

Restoring Pro on a new phone

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Tiltwake, then tap Restore Purchases in Settings or on the Pro screen.

Cancelling a subscription

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions → Tiltwake Pro → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
