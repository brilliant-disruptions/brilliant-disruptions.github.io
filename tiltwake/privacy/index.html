Tiltwake Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Tiltwake is an alarm clock app made by Brilliant Disruptions. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. This page explains the little information that leaves your phone and why.

What stays on your phone

  • Your alarms, their labels and times, your settings, streaks and maze progress.
  • Motion data from tilting the phone. It is used live to roll the marble and is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • A random install number kept in your phone's Keychain, used only to keep in-app experiments (such as which price you see) consistent on your device. It is never sent to us.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn whether Tiltwake actually wakes people up, the app sends anonymous usage events through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. Examples: an alarm was scheduled, a maze was solved and how long it took, the emergency escape was used, a purchase or trial started.

  • Events never include your alarm labels, your name, contacts, location or anything you type.
  • TelemetryDeck receives a one-way hashed identifier, not your Apple Account, advertising ID or device name, and the data is not linked to you.
  • We use it only to improve the app. We don't sell it, share it with advertisers or use it to track you across other apps and websites.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Apple of which plan is active.

Notifications and alarms

Alarm and reminder permissions are used only to ring your alarms and send the reminders you turn on. They are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to a server.

Children

Tiltwake is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your phone, except the Keychain install number, which iOS removes when you reset the phone. Because the usage statistics are anonymous, we cannot connect them back to you to export or delete them. If you have a question, contact us and we'll help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
